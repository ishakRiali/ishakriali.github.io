I build knowledge graphs, Graph-RAG systems and ETL pipelines that turn messy, uncertain data into structured knowledge.

I combine Semantic Web technologies, probabilistic reasoning and Large Language Models into systems that are rigorous enough for research and robust enough for production, in any domain.

My Ph.D. (University of Blida 1) focused on modeling uncertain knowledge in the Semantic Web. I then spent five years as a university lecturer and data-center manager, teaching databases, logic and programming and supervising twelve Master's theses.

02 / Featured project

BecaGraph

A full-stack knowledge-graph platform that turns heterogeneous museum spreadsheets into one unified, uncertainty-aware and queryable graph.

ROLEDesigned & developed end to end
CONTEXTBECACO project, Leiden University
SCALE530K+ objects, 11 museums
STACKPython, Django, GraphDB, SPARQL, RDF-Star, SHACL, LLMs, Transformers, WebGL
  1. 01Semantic ETL pipelineExports from several collection systems mapped to CIDOC CRM, with LLM-assisted event extraction from free text and SHACL validation.
  2. 02Uncertainty-aware modelingQualifiers such as "Maya?" or "ca. 1880 to 1910" kept as RDF-Star annotations instead of being lost.
  3. 03Hybrid entity resolutionA RoBERTa Cross-Encoder combined with Token Sort Ratio merges name variants of the same collector across institutions.
  4. 04Ask the GraphA Graph-RAG agent that writes SPARQL, runs it read-only and answers with evidence, showing every step.
