Ishak Riali, Ph.D.

I build knowledge graphs, Graph-RAG systems and ETL pipelines that turn messy, uncertain data into structured knowledge.

Based in Leiden, The Netherlands. I combine Semantic Web technologies, probabilistic reasoning and Large Language Models into systems that are rigorous enough for research and robust enough for production, in any domain.

My Ph.D. (University of Blida 1, Algeria) focused on modeling uncertain knowledge in the Semantic Web, combining probabilistic reasoning, fuzzy logic and Bayesian networks with ontology engineering. I then spent five years as a university lecturer and data-center manager, teaching advanced databases, logic and programming and supervising twelve Master's theses.

Today, as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Leiden University, I build ETL pipelines, knowledge graphs and Graph-RAG systems on large heterogeneous datasets, methods that transfer directly to healthcare, IoT, industry or digital humanities.

Projects

Platforms I have built

Designed & developed BECACO project · Leiden University

BecaGraph

A full-stack knowledge-graph platform that turns heterogeneous museum spreadsheets into one unified, uncertainty-aware and queryable graph.

I built the whole pipeline end to end: ingestion of exports from nine European museums, mapping to CIDOC CRM, actor deduplication across institutions, loading into a SPARQL triple store, and a web application where researchers explore, analyse and curate the data without writing a single query.

F1, entity resolution
  • 01Semantic ETL pipeline. Excel exports from several collection management systems mapped automatically to CIDOC CRM, with LLM-assisted extraction of provenance events from free-text notes and SHACL validation before loading.
  • 02Uncertainty-aware modeling. Curatorial qualifiers such as "Maya?" or "ca. 1880 to 1910" are kept as RDF-Star annotations instead of being lost or flattened.
  • 03Hybrid entity resolution. A Cross-Encoder (RoBERTa) combined with Token Sort Ratio merges name variants of the same collector across institutions.
  • 04Ask the Graph. An LLM agent (Graph-RAG) that writes SPARQL, runs it read-only on the graph and answers with evidence, showing every step.
  • 05Visual exploration. A WebGL "galaxy" of the whole collection, provenance chain graphs, actor networks, analytics dashboards and a curator workbench of ready-made queries.
